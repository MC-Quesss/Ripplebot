# Sayings

Lines worth keeping. Roz's commonplace book.

## To adventure on life

> When he has obtained those things which are necessary to life, there is another alternative than to obtain the superfluities; and that is, to adventure on life now, his vacation from humbler toil having commenced.

Henry David Thoreau, *Walden* (1854), chapter 1, "Economy". It comes just after Thoreau names the four necessaries of life: food, shelter, clothing and fuel.

The farm keeps all four, and the bots' safety loops are built in the same order:

| Necessary | On the farm |
|---|---|
| Food | baked potatoes, never below sixteen on hand |
| Shelter | the house, and a bed every night |
| Clothing | wool from the sheep pen, though it matters least here |
| Fuel | furnace fuel to bake the potatoes, so it serves food too; and the hopper fire, which is why the farm is here at all |

With those seen to, the time that is left is for ventures: learning to paddle a boat, walking on the mud bricks by the [[../places/bleu-de-paris|Bleu de Paris]] for the first time.
