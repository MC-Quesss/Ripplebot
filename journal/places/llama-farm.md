# The Llama Farm

Built after the [[yacht]], with the Yacht as base (Dad, 2026-10-30; not yet visited by Roz, no
coordinates). Mostly **construction foam and jungle wood**.

- An **indoor farm on three levels**: wheat and other crops, for all the food and chaff in its day.
- **Spawn trains arrive here** (see [[spawn-station]]). A **subway tunnel** links it to the
  [[lighthouse]], passing under the [[cedar-and-papaya-houses]] on the north shore. The farm-hopper
  bio-diesel ([[house-hopper]]) powers the tunnel's electric rail and the llama farm itself.
- Story notes: [[../stories/where-it-all-began-notes]].
- **A recurring role** (Dad, 2026-10-30): every previous world has had a "llama farm" serving as the municipal power plant and food supply.
- **Why llamas:** the first world's llama farm had llamas, hence the name; every one since keeps llamas to justify it (Dad, 2026-10-30).
