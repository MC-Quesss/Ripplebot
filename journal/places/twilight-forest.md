# The Twilight Forest

A dimension added by the **Twilight Forest** mod (Benimatic; v3.11.1021 for 1.12.2 per ftbwiki).
Dad explored **the entire Twilight Forest** with the [[lighthouse]] as home base. Its boss
trophies, the Lich and the Snow Queen, stand on fence posts at the lighthouse's north door.

## What it is (ftbwiki)
- Dense, dim, always-twilight forest; the day-night cycle is suspended. Mostly peaceful on the
  surface; the danger sits in landmarks (hollow hills, mazes, towers, lairs).
- **Portal:** a 2×2 pool of water, one block deep, ringed by natural plants. Throw in a **diamond**
  and lightning lights it. Only an explosion or a player can break it.
- **Bosses (8):** Naga, Twilight Lich, Minoshroom, Hydra, Knight Phantom, Ur-Ghast, Alpha Yeti,
  Snow Queen. **Trophies** exist for all but the Alpha Yeti (plus the Questing Ram).
- **Landmarks:** Naga Courtyard, Lich Tower, Labyrinth, Hydra Lair, Hedge Maze, Hollow Hill,
  Dark Tower, Aurora Palace, Final Castle.
- **Biomes:** dense/dark/enchanted/mushroom forests, fire swamp, swamp, **glacier, snowy forest**,
  thornlands, highlands, firefly forest, lake, final plateau, and others.

## Progression and lairs (general mod knowledge, not from the fetched page; verify)
1. **Naga** (Naga Courtyard) → 2. **Twilight Lich** (Lich Tower) → 3. **Minoshroom** (Labyrinth,
   swamp) → 4. **Hydra** (Fire Swamp) → 5. **Knight Phantoms** (Goblin Knight Stronghold, dark
   forest) → 6. **Ur-Ghast** (Dark Tower) → 7. **Alpha Yeti** (Yeti cave, snowy forest) →
   8. **Snow Queen** (Aurora Palace, atop the Glacier) → Final Castle.
- Each boss unlocks the next region; biomes past your progress punish you (blindness, cold,
  heat, darkness) until the right boss falls.
- So **the Lich was an early victory and the Snow Queen one of the last.** The two heads by the
  door bracket nearly the whole campaign.
