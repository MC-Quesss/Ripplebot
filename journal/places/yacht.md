# The Yacht (western point of Oceanside)

Dad's **first BIG project** in Oceanside, after the [[cedar-and-papaya-houses]] (Dad, 2026-10-30;
not yet visited by Roz, no coordinates). Built of **construction foam**, a byproduct of washing
ores, chosen because it scaled better than wood. Every foam block was made at the [[lighthouse]].

**Not the same boat as the [[bleu-de-paris]]** (the river boat by the farm). Dad's boats in order:
the sailboat at the lighthouse, then the Yacht, then the Bleu de Paris.

Story notes: [[../stories/where-it-all-began-notes]].

## Systems (Dad, 2026-10-30)
- **10-fermenter bio-diesel engine** (power plant), **2 macerators**, furnace upgrades, a "super workshop".
- **Ore washing** on board, so it makes its own construction foam.
- Aboard: a **small garden** and a **full galley**; not enough wheat for chaff at scale, hence the [[llama-farm]] (Dad, 2026-10-30).
- Luxury: **gold railings**, a **regeneration bath** in the master suite (Dad, 2026-10-30). Too grand to feel like home; Dad lives on the [[bleu-de-paris]] instead.
