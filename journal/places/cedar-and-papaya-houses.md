# Cedar House and Papaya House (north shore of Oceanside)

Dad's first two houses in **Oceanside**, after the [[mud-hut|Loam Lodge]] and the [[lighthouse]]
(Dad, 2026-10-30; not yet visited by Roz, no coordinates).

- **Cedar house:** the first house built in Oceanside. Dad didn't yet know how to get more cedar,
  so it was only just finished when the cedar ran out.
- **Papaya house:** papaya wood, built next to the cedar house.
- Both stand on the **northern shore of Oceanside**. The **subway** runs underneath them to the
  llama farm (where spawn trains arrive). See [[spawn-station]].
