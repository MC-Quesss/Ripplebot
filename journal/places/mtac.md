# MTAC — Mike Tomlin Aquatic Center

Quesss's new construction project beside the [[farm-port]]. On 2026-10-24 it was mocked up in **dirt**, and Quesss said it won't stay dirt. So find it by these coordinates, not by block type.

## First survey — 2026-10-24 (day 56409), from `find_blocks dirt`

Roz measured it from the wheat field just after landing at the port. The ground there is dirt at y 62, so the mock-up starts at y 63.

- **Footprint: about 19 × 16**. x -277 … -259 (19 blocks east-west), z 527 … 542 (16 blocks north-south).
- **Height: up to y 75**, which is 13 layers above the ground (y 63–75).
- **Tall posts reaching y 75:** the east corners (-259, 527), (-259, 537) and (-259, 542). There are two pairs on the south wall: (-272/-271, 542) and (-265/-264, 542), which may frame an entrance or windows. There's also a cluster at the north side around (-264…-263, 526…528). The west wall's posts are shorter (-277, 537 to y 72; -277, 542 to y 73).
- **Walls at y 63–64:** the south wall z 542 and east wall x -259 run full length, and the west wall is x -277/-276. The north edge at z 527–531 is filled solid at y 63, which may be a floor or a deck.
- **A spur running north** from the north wall toward the port: columns at x -264 and x -269, z 521…527, rising to about y 69–72.

## Caveats

- The survey can't tell building dirt from natural dirt at ground level (y 63–64), and the search hit its 5000-block cap. Treat the outline as approximate until Roz walks it or Quesss confirms the numbers.
- Unverified whether the dirt patch at x -258…-252, z 546…560 is part of the plan or just terrain.

## Update — 2026-11-07: no longer dirt (day 57791)

Roz surveyed it by block reads from the path at (-269, 63, 545.5), outside the building, covering x -280…-256, z 524…544, y 62…83. It is unfinished, and Quesss intends the upper part as a hotel, with a pool below and a basement water pump used to make concrete.

- **Materials read:** glass (type 20) about 35 blocks per layer at y 66–74, concrete (251) from y 62 up, water (9) at y 62–64, glass panes (102), wool (35) at y 77–80, wall banners (177), and many modded types with empty names.
- **Type 690 runs through the frame** from y 65 to at least y 83. It matches the type of the trunk read at the [[meadow-bee-cross]] on 2026-11-06, which fits the cedar coming from there, as Quesss says.
- **Type 4029 appears on most layers.** At the [[ocean-cabin]] that type behaves as an invisible wall, so do not assume open-looking spaces here are walkable.
- **Limits:** the survey hit its edges (x -280, z 544, y 83), so the building may extend beyond them. Bounds in the frontmatter date from the dirt mock-up. Roz has not been inside.
