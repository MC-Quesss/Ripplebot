# The Loam Lodge (a.k.a. the Mud Hut) — where it all began

Dad's first home in this world, from its very first night: a hole in the swamp where gravel
had collapsed, roofed with mud since gravel won't hold. Later the mud became mud bricks, his
first building material (the same bricks lie by the [[bleu-de-paris]]). Visited by boat from
the [[farm-port]] on 2026-10-30. Story notes: [[../stories/where-it-all-began-notes]].

  reports "no dry landing within 4 blocks", but standing in the water is safe.

- **Doorway (where Dad stood, 2026-10-30):** at (184, 64, 61) facing west, on top of the gravel.
  Mossy cobblestone walls along x 185 (y 65–66). Modded block type 3111 in the doorway (184, 64, 61),

  **Wooden pressure plate** just inside at (185, 64, 61). **Wall sign** at (184, 65, 62); the bot can't
