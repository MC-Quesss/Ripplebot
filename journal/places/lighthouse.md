# The Lighthouse

Dad's **second home**, after the [[mud-hut|Loam Lodge]]. It began as a bare frozen beach on the
northwest corner of the land that now holds the [[meadow-bee-cross|bee cross]] and the
[[bee-cabin]]. The first bee cross stood here. Toured 2026-10-30
([[../stories/where-it-all-began-notes]]).

## History (Dad, 2026-10-30)
- At first the **east wall was eight furnaces**, all smelting stone at once to build the tower.
- When the original structure was finished, that wall was opened out into the **fireplace and
  foyer**. The lighthouse **jukebox** is there.
